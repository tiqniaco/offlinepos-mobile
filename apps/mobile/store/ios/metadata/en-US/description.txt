Kashiro is your shop on your phone: sell by barcode in seconds, keep stock right, track customer and supplier balances, and see clear reports — all working offline.

• Fast checkout
Scan barcodes with the camera or a scanner; take cash, card, wallet or credit. Print on a Bluetooth printer or send the invoice as a PDF on WhatsApp.

• Stock at a glance
Products with units (piece / carton), low-stock alerts, stock counts, and Excel import.

• Customers & suppliers
Balances, statements for any period, collections and payments, and WhatsApp reminders for late payers.

• Purchases, expenses & shifts
Purchase invoices add stock and update cost; expense types; close shifts with shortage and overage.

• Offers & coupons
Percentage discounts, buy 2 get 1, quantity pricing, and coupon codes.

• Reports
Sales, profit, stock and debt aging — PDF, Excel and print.

• Your team
A PIN and permissions for every staff member, and extra cashier phones on the same shop.

• Secure
Data is encrypted on the phone, saved invoices can't be edited, and tampering is detected. Encrypted backups and easy moves to a new phone.

Built for shops in Egypt: Egyptian pounds and 14% VAT.
